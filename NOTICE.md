## Conversion

Originals are resized proportionally into 64px color bitmaps. They are not
traced, redrawn, or recolored. Identical pixels are embedded in sbix, CBDT/CBLC,
and OpenType-SVG encodings inside a single WOFF2 for browser compatibility.
This is a color bitmap font, not an infinitely scalable vector font.
